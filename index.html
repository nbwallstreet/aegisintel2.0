AEGIS Intelligence Engine

THE NINE PROPRIETARY
FRAMEWORKS

Each framework is a standalone analytical module. Together they form the complete AEGIS risk intelligence system. Click any card to view its full clinical write-up.

LEMS

Liability Exposure Mapping

Translates physical hazards directly into breach-of-statute risks under Ontario common law and the Occupiers' Liability Act.

VERMS

Vector Exposure Risk

Maps complex ecological, vector-borne, and hydrological runoff risks directly affecting property assets and waterfront compliance.

PINS

Property Inspection

Trace-verified, geo-tagged physical inspections of walkways, structures, stairs, and common areas with photographic evidence.

SEMS

Security Exposure Mapping

Identifies blind spots, access vulnerabilities, and lighting deficiencies across the entire property perimeter.

DDIS

Due Diligence Intelligence

Pre-acquisition liability mapping designed for real estate transaction velocity. Unvarnished latent risk before closing.

CPJS

Capital Project Justification

Models the exact 1, 3, and 5-year cost-of-inaction on deferred maintenance for board capital approval.

DOCS

Compliance Engine

Converts complex risk intelligence into daily operational SOPs, maintenance checklists, and audit-ready paper trails.

LARS

Labor Automation Engine

Our AI backend that compiles raw field findings into standardized, board-ready reports in minutes.

AECS

Contractor Estimating System

Integrates localized Ontario contractor cost-indexes to estimate remediation budgets instantly.
